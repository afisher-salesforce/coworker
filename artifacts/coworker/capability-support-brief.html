Support Engineer Brief (CRM-Native)

Equip frontline service teams with an instant pre-case briefing built from live CRM context, reducing preparation time and improving first-response quality.

"I am about to work a new enterprise support case. Summarize recent case history, open issues, and the last service interactions so I know where to focus first."

Faster case-readiness with less manual tab switching and less context loss between handoffs.
