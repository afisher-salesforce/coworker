Appendix

External Research

Blackbaud's current financial trajectory, market posture, and AI signals support a practical, controlled Coworker rollout aligned to operating priorities.

Company Profile and Scale

Blackbaud (NASDAQ: BLKB), founded in 1981 and headquartered in Charleston, serves social-impact organizations in more than 100 countries with approximately 3,400 employees.

Core Portfolio

Flagship products include Raiser's Edge NXT, Blackbaud CRM, Financial Edge NXT, SKY Platform, Payments, Grantmaking, and the agentic Agents for Good suite.

Strategic Positioning

Blackbaud has publicly positioned itself as an AI-forward leader in social impact, with increasing emphasis on intelligent action across its product stack.

Financial Trajectory

FY2025 revenue was approximately $1.12B (vs. ~$1.15B in FY2024), reflecting the EVERFI divestiture while maintaining scale and focus in the core portfolio.

Profitability and Model Quality

Blackbaud turned GAAP-profitable in FY2025 with a business model that remains predominantly recurring revenue (about 90%+), indicating durable operating leverage.

Capital Discipline Signals

Post-divestiture focus, active share repurchases, and margin-recovery messaging indicate disciplined portfolio management and an outcomes-oriented leadership stance.

Market Tailwinds

Demand for digital fundraising, nonprofit efficiency, CSR program growth, and agentic AI adoption all reinforce the business case for employee productivity acceleration.

Competitive Pressure

Blackbaud faces pressure from Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud, Microsoft ecosystems, and emerging nonprofit platforms, increasing urgency for differentiated execution speed.

AI and Transformation Signals

Agents for Good launches, broad embedded-AI investment, and SKY platform expansion show a clear direction toward operationalized AI, not isolated pilots.

Salesforce Account Implications

Why Coworker Fits Now

Blackbaud is already operating in an AI-adoption posture. Coworker enables immediate employee value in familiar workflows without a custom interface program.

Commercial and Delivery Relevance

A controlled pilot with support and renewal teams aligns to measurable near-term outcomes while preserving governance and consumption control.

Executive Discussion Focus

The strategic question is less platform viability and more activation sequencing: where to start, how to measure, and when to scale.
