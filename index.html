A Faster, Lower-Risk Path to Employee AI Execution

Agentforce Coworker gives Blackbaud a ready-now AI teammate surface inside Salesforce, and a headless path to Microsoft Teams, without a custom DIY architecture that delays value.

Grounded in CRM + Knowledge Routes to Existing Agentforce Agents Pilot-Ready in Weeks

Purpose of This Executive Discussion

Align Blackbaud business and technology leadership on when and how to operationalize Agentforce Coworker as the employee-facing interface for AI-enabled work across support, renewal, and account planning motions.

Strategic Decision

Shift from “should we build this ourselves?” to “how fast can we activate controlled value with governance and measurable outcomes?”

Business Impact

Reduce prep time, improve case and renewal readiness, and shorten time-to-action for frontline teams using natural language.

Delivery Model

Start in Salesforce where Coworker is already available, then extend to Teams through headless patterns as integration reaches GA milestones.

External Research: Blackbaud Context

The recommendation is grounded in Blackbaud's current market posture, operating model, and AI strategy.

Business Profile

  • Purpose-built social impact software leader with global reach.
  • Strong recurring-revenue model and cloud-first operating maturity.
  • Mission-critical footprint in nonprofit and education segments.

AI Direction

  • Public AI-forward positioning with active agentic initiatives.
  • Existing roadmap momentum supports an employee AI interface layer now.
  • Leadership messaging emphasizes responsible, outcomes-driven adoption.

Execution Reality

  • Teams need practical value before broad-scale change commitments.
  • Contact center and renewal programs demand cross-object intelligence.
  • Controlled pilot model fits COE governance expectations.

Capability 1: Support Engineer Brief (CRM-Native)

Prompt

“I’m a support engineer about to work a new enterprise case. Summarize recent case history, open issues, and the last service interactions so I know where to focus.”

Persona

Support Engineer, Support Manager, Contact Center Leader

Business Outcome

Faster case readiness and reduced swivel-chair analysis across records.

Business Value

Improves time-to-resolution and consistency during support interactions while maximizing existing Service Cloud investments.

Capability 2: Knowledge-Grounded Answers

Prompt

“What are the documented best practices for this issue category? Show the relevant internal knowledge guidance and recommended resolution pattern.”

Persona

Support Engineer, CSM, Implementation Consultant

Business Outcome

Answers grounded in institutional knowledge, not generic AI speculation.

Business Value

Increases trust, reduces rework, and unlocks higher return on prior knowledge-management investments.

Capability 3: Stakeholder & Relationship Mapping

Prompt

“Identify the key decision stakeholders, latest engagement history, and coverage gaps ahead of the renewal conversation.”

Persona

Renewal Manager, AE, Account Team Leader

Business Outcome

Improved executive-call preparedness and more targeted relationship actions.

Business Value

Reduces revenue risk by exposing relationship blind spots before critical renewal milestones.

Capability 4: Grounded Read-to-Write Delegated Action

Prompt

“Review this at-risk renewal using playbook guidance, then delegate an update to the appropriate agent to flag risk and set next action.”

Persona

Renewal Manager, Sales Manager, Revenue Operations

Business Outcome

Moves from insight to execution in one flow: retrieve, reason, and act.

Business Value

Compresses cycle time from diagnosis to intervention, improving consistency in at-risk account handling.
